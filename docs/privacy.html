Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 27, 2026

This policy explains how Tired CapyBob Uploader (the "App") handles Google account information. The App is a personal tool the channel owner uses to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

1. Information we use

  • Access and refresh tokens obtained through Google OAuth sign-in
  • Basic information about the signed-in account's YouTube channel (channel ID, channel name)
  • Video files being uploaded, with their title, description, tags and privacy setting
  • Statistics for videos the owner uploaded (views, likes, average view duration, audience retention)

2. How we use it

This information is used only to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and to review upload results and the performance of the owner's own videos to improve future videos. The App does not access data of other users or other channels, and does not use the information for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

3. Storage and retention

Tokens are stored only in a local configuration file on the owner's personal computer and are never sent to a separate server or database. Tokens are deleted when the owner stops using the App or revokes access. Video statistics are kept only as local files on the owner's computer and are deleted or refreshed from the YouTube API within 30 days.

4. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell or share collected information with third parties. Information is sent only to Google (YouTube) for uploading.

5. YouTube API Services

The App uses the YouTube API Services. By using the App, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of personal information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Revoking access

You can revoke the App's access at any time from your Google Account security settings.

7. Data deletion

Deleting the local configuration and statistics files immediately removes all information kept by the App. To request deletion, contact the email below. We will delete the data within 7 days of receiving the request.
